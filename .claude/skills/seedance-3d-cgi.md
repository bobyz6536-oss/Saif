## The 2-Second Hook Framework for 3D CGI

Every successful CGI video begins with an immediate visual hook—something that stops the viewer's eye in the first two seconds. The subsequent 8 seconds develop the hook into a complete narrative or reveal.

### 10+ Hook Techniques for 3D CGI

#### 1. **Dramatic Camera Fly-Through**
The camera moves through impossible geometry at high speed. Used for: sci-fi spaces, architectural visualization, abstract environments, tunnel sequences.

#### 2. **Scale Reveal (Tiny to Massive)**
Start with an extreme closeup—a water droplet, a grain of sand, a single fiber—then pull back to reveal it's part of something enormous.

#### 3. **Impossible Camera Angle**
Utilize camera positions that human cameras can't occupy: inside glass, on the surface tension of water, rotating through a solid object's cross-section.

#### 4. **Object Materializing from Particles**
Begin with thousands of small particles (dust, light, pixels, data) that coalesce into a recognizable 3D object.

#### 5. **Photorealistic Object in Surreal Setting**
Place a hyper-real object in an impossible or dreamlike environment.

#### 6. **Transformative Morph Sequence**
One object smoothly transitions into another, often defying physics.

#### 7. **Revealing Through Transparency**
Start with a solid opaque object, then it becomes transparent or reflective, revealing internal structure.

#### 8. **Depth Shift with Depth of Field**
Begin with extreme DOF. Shift focus to reveal background, midground, or foreground in crisp detail.

#### 9. **Chromatic Separation & Refraction**
Light bends, splits, or refracts, creating rainbow effects and optical distortion.

#### 10. **Kinetic Decomposition**
A structured object disassembles into floating components, revealing internal mechanisms or beauty.

#### 11. **Environmental Reaction**
A character or object enters an environment that reacts to it—water ripples, particles scatter, wind displacement.

#### 12. **Temporal Speed Manipulation**
The video alternates between slow-motion and fast-motion, or shows the same motion at different speeds.

## Render Styles

- **Pixar/Stylized**: Warm, saturated colors; smooth shading; exaggerated proportions
- **Photorealistic**: Pixel-perfect simulation; accurate material behavior; complex lighting
- **Low-Poly 3D**: Geometric, faceted forms; flat colors; minimalist geometry
- **Claymation/Plasticine**: Organic, handmade aesthetic; tactile surface quality
- **Isometric 3D**: Perfect 30–45-degree angle; geometric clarity
- **Cel-Shaded 3D**: Comic book aesthetic; bold outlines; limited color palette
- **Voxel 3D**: Pixelated, cube-based; Minecraft-inspired
- **Wireframe & Technical**: Skeleton-only geometry; neon glowing lines
- **Hybrid/Mixed Media**: Combine photorealistic with stylized elements

## Material Science & Surface Properties

- **Subsurface Scattering (SSS)**: Light penetrates translucent surfaces (skin, wax, marble, leaves)
- **Physically Based Rendering (PBR)**: Surfaces follow real physics; metallic/roughness workflows
- **Metallic Finishes**: Polished, brushed, oxidized, anodized
- **Glass & Crystal**: Clear, frosted, colored, optical, crystal faceted
- **Fabric & Cloth**: Woven texture, dynamic wrinkles, yarn-level detail
- **Fur & Hair**: Individual strand detail, flowing dynamics
- **Water & Liquid**: Caustics, refraction, surface tension, splash dynamics
- **Skin**: Pore detail, SSS, micro-texture, natural color variation
- **Wood**: Grain patterns, growth rings, weathering
- **Organic Surfaces**: Bark, stone, scales, feathers

## Lighting Setup Language

- **HDRI**: Environment lighting from 360-degree photograph
- **3-Point Lighting**: Key (main), fill (secondary), back/rim
- **Global Illumination (GI)**: Indirect light bouncing between surfaces
- **Caustics**: Light refracting through water or glass
- **God Rays (Volumetric Light)**: Light beams through dust/fog/atmosphere
- **Color Temperature**: Warm (3200K orange) vs cool (8000K+ blue)

## Camera Movement for CGI

- **Slow Orbital Rotation** — 360° turntable around subject
- **Ascending Crane Shot** — upward and backward, revealing scale
- **Push-In / Dolly Forward** — moves toward subject, DOF shifts
- **Tracking Lateral** — slides left or right across scene
- **Reveal Pan** — horizontal pan uncovering hidden information
- **Spiral Ascent** — orbit + upward movement simultaneously
- **Glide-Through** — moves through physical space (tunnel, corridor)
- **Dolly Zoom** — dolly forward + focal length change (vertigo effect)
- **Floating Free Camera** — moves freely in 3D space, dreamlike
- **Focus-Shift Dolly** — position static, DOF shifts foreground→background
- **Easing In/Out** — accelerates gradually, decelerates at end (essential)
- **Circular Orbit at Variable Distance** — orbit + distance change simultaneously

## Common Mistakes & Fixes

| ❌ Mistake | ✓ Fix |
|---|---|
| "Dynamic camera movement" | "Slow orbital rotation at 45°/sec, easing motion, 24fps" |
| "Every atom visible" | "Hyperdetailed textures down to microscopic level" |
| "Bright shadows" | "Deep shadows from single key, 2:1 contrast ratio with fill" |
| "Velvet with mirror reflections" | "Black velvet nap with diffuse scatter, zero specularity" |
| "Lots of cool particles flowing" | "Thousands of cyan particles following spline path, 5cm spacing, fading over 1s" |
| No duration specified | Always add "Duration: X seconds" |
| "Photorealistic but also stylized" | Specify which elements are realistic vs. stylized explicitly |
| Weak hook | Make first instant visually arresting — glow, impossible physics, scale shock |
