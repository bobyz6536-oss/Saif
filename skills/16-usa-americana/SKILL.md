## Overview

Generate cinematic, emotionally resonant videos celebrating American landscapes, culture, heritage, and daily life. From the Grand Canyon at golden hour to neon-lit diners on Route 66, this skill captures the breadth and soul of the United States — wide-open spaces, urban energy, patriotic pride, and authentic human stories.

## Camera Encyclopedia

### Movement Techniques
- **Hyperlapse drone arc** — circles a monument or city block as day becomes night
- **Low-rider dolly** — ground-level push along Route 66 asphalt toward vanishing point
- **Tilt-shift top-down** — miniaturizes a city grid or ballpark
- **Whip pan cut** — swipes between iconic city skylines (NYC → Chicago → LA)
- **Helicopter spiral descent** — corkscrews down onto Times Square or the White House lawn
- **Truck shot** — camera travels laterally alongside a freight train crossing the plains
- **Crash zoom** — slams into a Fourth of July firework burst at peak bloom
- **Underwater rise** — camera ascends from below surface of a Florida reef
- **Slow push on face** — gentle dolly toward a veteran's face at a memorial
- **Follow cam run** — GoPro-style pursuit through Central Park autumn leaves
- **360° turntable** — subject rotates against an iconic backdrop (Arches NP, Hollywood sign)
- **Boom crane rise** — lifts off a rural porch to reveal endless Iowa cornfields

### Lens & Depth
- **Ultra-wide 14mm** — exaggerates prairie skies and monument scale
- **Telephoto compression** — stacks city buildings or mountain ridgelines
- **Macro detail** — Constitution parchment text, bald eagle feathers, cornbread crust
- **Anamorphic lens flare** — golden-hour sun streaks across the Pacific Coast Highway
- **Tilt-shift selective focus** — isolates a single person in a crowded Times Square

### Lighting Moods
- **Magic hour amber** — desert buttes glow copper at sunset
- **Overcast patriot grey** — somber tone for veteran or memorial content
- **Neon night rain** — wet city streets reflect pink and blue signs
- **Midday heat shimmer** — asphalt mirage on Nevada highways
- **Firework strobe** — rapid burst lighting for Independence Day sequences
- **Northern lights** — Alaska aurora illuminates snow-covered wilderness

## Example Prompts

### Example 1 — Grand Canyon Sunrise Reveal (Cinematic / Travel)

```
A pre-dawn desert plateau, total darkness except for distant orange glow on the horizon.
Camera is ground-level at canyon rim, lens pointing east.
A slow push forward begins as the first gold light crests the horizon,
casting long shadows across the layered red and ochre canyon walls below.
The Colorado River glimmers silver in the depths, thousands of feet down.
Drone lifts off the rim in a sweeping arc, revealing the full 277-mile canyon breadth.
Anamorphic lens captures sun-ray flares as the sun clears the opposite rim.
A solo hiker appears in silhouette at the rim, arms outstretched.
Timelapse sky overhead: rose → amber → piercing blue in seconds.
Cut to extreme close-up of canyon rock striations — geological time made visible.
Pull back to widest possible aerial establishing shot: human scale obliterated by nature.
Ending title card fades in over the canyon: "America the Beautiful."
Photorealistic 8K rendering, cinematic color grade, Dolby Vision HDR.
```

### Example 2 — Fourth of July Fireworks (Patriotic / Celebration)

```
Night, Washington D.C. The National Mall is packed with a hundred thousand people.
Camera opens on the Washington Monument reflection shimmering in the Reflecting Pool.
A distant boom — the first firework chrysanthemum explodes gold above the Lincoln Memorial.
Slow-motion crowd reaction: upturned faces, open mouths, children on shoulders.
Telephoto compression stacks six firework bursts simultaneously above the Capitol dome.
Ground-level wide shot: sparklers streak across frame as families wave American flags.
Crash zoom into a single red-white-blue starburst at peak bloom — fills the entire frame.
Drone ascent to 500 feet: the Mall transforms into a river of light and color.
Rain of glowing embers falls in slow motion over the Washington Monument.
Close-up: a veteran's face, tear on cheek, American flag pin on lapel, soft smile.
Final wide shot: the full D.C. skyline silhouetted against a sky full of color.
Fade to: a child's drawing of a firework on the screen — simple, joyful, pure.
Cinematic color grade, practical firework light sources, 120fps slow-motion inserts.
```

### Example 3 — Route 66 Road Trip (Americana / Nostalgia)

```
Dawn. A vintage 1967 Ford Mustang fastback, cherry red, idles on a desert highway.
Empty road stretches to a vanishing point flanked by red mesas and sagebrush.
Camera tracks low alongside the rear quarter panel as the engine revs and tires roll.
Montage of Route 66 icons: Wigwam Motel cabins, Blue Swallow neon sign, Cadillac Ranch.
Interior shot: driver's hand on white-knuckle steering wheel, turquoise ring, worn leather glove.
Roadside diner — chrome stools, pie under glass dome, waitress pouring black coffee.
Timelapse: storm clouds build over Monument Valley; lightning forks behind the Mittens.
Night. Neon signs of a forgotten town reflected in rain-slicked blacktop.
Stereo speakers mount on a drive-in movie screen showing a classic Western.
Sunrise again — a new day, a new state line. "Welcome to Texas" sign passes in b.g.
Final aerial: the red Mustang becomes a tiny moving dot in an infinite desert.
Super: "3,940 miles. One road. One nation." fade to black.
Film grain texture, Kodachrome-inspired color palette, anamorphic aspect ratio 2.39:1.
```

### Example 4 — New York City Hustle (Urban / Energy)

```
5:58 AM. A single street light on an empty Midtown Manhattan block.
Steam rises from a grate. A lone hot dog cart is pushed into position.
Cut: 6:01 AM — the first commuters emerge from a subway entrance like a river.
Match cut: river of people → timelapse of rush hour reaching full density.
Helicopter shot descending the length of 5th Avenue at peak morning traffic.
Close-ups rapid-fire: briefcase clasps, coffee lids snapping, MetroCard swipes.
A street performer's saxophone fills the air in Grand Central's Main Concourse.
Golden shaft of light through a Grand Central skylight — commuters walk through it.
Time-lapse sunset: Hudson River turns molten gold; skyline shifts from amber to purple.
Night: Times Square in full neon glory, 360-degree view from the center of an intersection.
Final shot: the Empire State Building crown transitions from red-white-blue to all-white.
Text overlay: "8.3 million stories. One city."
Handheld energy, quick cuts (1.5–2s), desaturated base with punchy color pops, 4K.
```

### Example 5 — Yellowstone Wildlife & Wilderness (Nature / Wonder)

```
Pre-dawn Yellowstone. Thermal steam drifts across a mirror-still lake at 28°F.
A lone bison bull materializes from the mist, breath fogging, hooves crunching frost.
Camera tracks at ground level alongside the bison — eye contact, life in those dark eyes.
Old Faithful begins its pre-eruption tremor; crowd hushes; camera tilts up.
Eruption: a 185-foot column of boiling water and steam launches skyward in slow motion.
180° pan across the Grand Prismatic Spring — electric cobalt, chartreuse, tangerine rings.
Eagle's eye drone: the Yellowstone River bends through a painted canyon of gold and rust.
A grizzly sow and two cubs emerge from a tree line, pause, then lumber toward a stream.
Underwater shot: a cutthroat trout hangs motionless in crystal-clear current.
Twilight: the Milky Way core rises over the Tetons, reflected in the Snake River bend.
Time-lapse of an entire day: geysers, herds, light changes, moon arc — compressed to 12 seconds.
Final wide: a ranger in silhouette watches the last light leave the sky.
Photorealistic nature documentary grade, Sony Venice color science, Dolby Atmos audio cues.
```

### Example 6 — American Dream Story (Inspirational / Brand Narrative)

```

Corner store owner flips the "Open" sign at 6 AM, pride on her face.
Construction worker at the top of a steel beam, city stretching in every direction.
Firefighter emerging from smoke, helmet tilted, holding a rescued child.
Farmer's market: hands exchanging produce, eye contact, community.
Slow push into a classroom chalkboard: "We the People…"
Final shot: an American flag on a front porch catches the morning breeze.
Voice-over placeholder: "This is your story. This is America."
Warm documentary-style grade, handheld intimacy, natural light throughout.
```
